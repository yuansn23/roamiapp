Free eSIM Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about frequently asked questions regarding free digital SIMs to help you easily start your seamless network experience.

Is the free eSIM really free?
Yes, our free eSIM trial plan is completely free. You don't need to link a credit card, and there are no hidden fees. We hope this allows you to experience our premium network service before purchasing a larger data plan.

Which countries does the free eSIM support?
Currently, our free eSIM supports multiple popular global travel and business destinations, including the United States, Japan, South Korea, various European countries, and Southeast Asia. You can find your desired destination in the list above.

How much data does the free eSIM come with?
The free trial plan typically includes 100MB of high-speed data, designed specifically for you to contact family, book a ride, or check maps as soon as you arrive at your destination.

How long is the free eSIM valid for?
The validity of the free eSIM is 7 days after activation. If you enjoy the network experience, you can seamlessly upgrade to a long-term, high-capacity plan on our platform at any time.

How do I install the free eSIM?
After successfully claiming it on the App Store, you will receive an email containing a QR code. Simply go to your phone's 'Settings' > 'Cellular/Mobile Data' > 'Add eSIM', and scan the QR code to complete the installation. The whole process takes just 1 minute.

Does the free eSIM support mobile hotspot sharing?
Yes. You can share your free eSIM data via your phone's mobile hotspot with your other devices (like tablets, laptops) or travel companions.

Can the free eSIM be used for phone calls and SMS?
The free eSIM only provides data services and does not include traditional voice calls or SMS. However, you can easily use network-based apps like WhatsApp, Skype, FaceTime, or WeChat for voice and video calls.

What if my device is not compatible?
Before claiming, please ensure your phone supports eSIM functionality (most newer models like iPhone 17, iPhone 16, Samsung Galaxy, and Google Pixel are supported). You can click to view our Device Compatibility List. If your device is not supported, you will not be able to install and use this service.

Can the free eSIM be upgraded to a paid plan?
Absolutely! When your free data is depleted or expires, you don't need to install a new physical SIM. Simply purchase a data package for the corresponding country on our website, and it will be directly recharged to your existing digital SIM for continued use.
