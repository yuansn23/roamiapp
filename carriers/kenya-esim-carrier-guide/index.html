Things you must know before buying a Kenya eSIM

Check your device compatibility

Before purchasing a Kenya eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern devices from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. You can verify your device’s support on our eSIM compatibility page.

Choose the right plan for your trip

Kenya eSIM plans are typically data-only. If you need a local number for calls or SMS, consider purchasing a physical SIM upon arrival. For most travelers, a data-only eSIM from Safaricom or Airtel is sufficient for navigation, messaging apps, and social media.

Activation and setup tips

Install the eSIM profile before you travel. Once you land in Kenya, enable data roaming and the eSIM will automatically connect to the local network. No physical SIM swap is required. Keep your primary SIM active for calls if needed.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Kenya eSIM

❌ Myth: You need a Kenyan phone number to use mobile data.
✅ Fact: Data-only eSIMs work perfectly for internet access. You don’t need a local number for WhatsApp, maps, or browsing.
❌ Myth: Safaricom is the only network that works in national parks.
✅ Fact: While Safaricom has the best coverage, Airtel also provides 4G in some parks like Amboseli and Tsavo West, though with weaker signal.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are expensive compared to physical SIMs.
✅ Fact: eSIM plans are competitively priced and often cheaper than international roaming. They also save you the hassle of finding a physical SIM vendor.
❌ Myth: You need to register your eSIM with the Kenyan government.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are pre-activated and comply with local regulations. No additional registration is required by the user.
❌ Myth: Kenya’s mobile network is too slow for video calls.
✅ Fact: Both Safaricom and Airtel support 4G/5G speeds sufficient for HD video calls in urban areas. In remote areas, speeds may drop but still support voice calls and messaging.
