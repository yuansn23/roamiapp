Australia eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Is eSIM compatible with all phones in Australia? ▼
    Most modern smartphones support eSIM, including iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer. Check your device’s eSIM compatibility before purchasing.
  • Q2: Can I keep my home number while using an Australia eSIM? ▼
    Yes, if your phone supports dual SIM. Use your physical SIM for calls and SMS from your home number, and the eSIM for data. This helps avoid roaming charges for data.
  • Q3: How do I activate my Australia eSIM? ▼
    After purchase, you’ll receive a QR code or activation code via email. On arrival in Australia, scan the QR code in your phone’s settings or enter the code manually. Enable data roaming, and you’re connected. No APN changes needed.
  • Q4: Which network is best for remote areas like Uluru? ▼
    Telstra has the widest coverage in remote and rural Australia, including Uluru, the Outback, and Tasmania. It is the most reliable choice for travelers venturing off the beaten path.
  • Q5: Will I experience throttled speeds after using a certain amount of data? ▼
    Some plans have fair usage policies. Check the specific plan details before purchase. Typically, after exceeding a high-speed data cap, speeds may be reduced to 128 kbps or 256 kbps, enough for messaging and basic browsing.

✅ Fact: eSIMs often offer competitive pricing, especially for short-term tourists. They eliminate roaming fees and the hassle of finding a physical SIM store. Many plans are cheaper than daily roaming charges.

✅ Fact: Most modern smartphones support eSIM. iPhones from XS onwards, Google Pixel 3+, and Samsung Galaxy S20+ are compatible. Check eSIM compatibility for your specific model.
