Spain eSIM FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about staying connected on your Spanish adventure.

Which Spanish network has the fastest 5G for streaming in cities?
According to Opensignal’s latest report, Movistar typically leads in 5G download speeds in urban areas like Madrid and Barcelona, making it the best choice for the fastest Spain eSIM experience for video streaming.

Is it better to buy a SIM at the airport or get an eSIM for Spain?
While you can buy a physical SIM at kiosks in airports like Barcelona-El Prat, purchasing a prepaid Spain eSIM online beforehand saves time, avoids language barriers, and activates instantly upon arrival.

Do Spain eSIM plans automatically switch between Movistar and Vodafone?
Yes, many modern Spain eSIM providers offer multi-network profiles that can automatically select the strongest available signal from Movistar, Vodafone, or Orange as you travel, ensuring optimal coverage.

Will I have internet on Spain's high-speed AVE trains?
Yes, but coverage varies. Vodafone and Orange generally provide the most consistent high-speed data connection on the AVE network, allowing you to work or stream reliably. Buying a dedicated Spain eSIM is far superior to relying on spotty train Wi-Fi.
