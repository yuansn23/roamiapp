Malta eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use a Malta eSIM on my iPhone? ▼
    Yes, iPhone models from XR onwards support eSIM compatibility. This includes iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and SE (2nd gen and later).
  • Q2: Will I lose my home number when using a Malta eSIM? ▼
    No. The eSIM works alongside your physical SIM. You can keep your home number active for calls and SMS while using the eSIM for data.
  • Q3: How do I activate the Malta eSIM? ▼
    After purchase, you’ll receive a QR code. Go to Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan on iPhone, or the equivalent on Android, and scan the QR code. Then enable data roaming upon arrival in Malta.
  • Q4: Which Malta carrier provides the best coverage in Gozo? ▼
    GO has the most extensive network on Gozo, covering even remote farmhouses and coastal paths. Epic is also available but with slightly less rural coverage.
  • Q5: Is there a 5G Malta eSIM option? ▼
    Yes, both GO and Epic offer 5G connectivity in major cities and towns. Your eSIM plan will automatically connect to 5G where available.
  • Q6: Can I top up my Malta eSIM if I run out of data? ▼
    Absolutely. You can purchase additional data directly from your Roami account. The top-up is applied instantly without needing a new eSIM.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Malta eSIM

❌ Myth: Malta is so small that any network works everywhere.
✅ Fact: Coverage varies significantly. GO has the best rural and island (Gozo, Comino) coverage, while Epic excels in cities. Choosing the wrong operator can mean weak signal in remote areas.
❌ Myth: You need a physical SIM because eSIMs are not reliable in Malta.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are fully supported by both GO and Epic. They offer the same network quality as physical SIMs and are more convenient for travelers.
❌ Myth: Free public Wi-Fi in Valletta is enough; you don’t need mobile data.
✅ Fact: Public Wi-Fi is often slow, insecure, and limited to tourist zones. A Malta eSIM gives you fast, private data everywhere – from Mdina to the Blue Grotto.
❌ Myth: All eSIMs for Malta use the same network.
✅ Fact: No. Roami partners with both GO and Epic, which are separate MNOs with different coverage footprints. Your choice affects your experience in rural vs. urban areas.
❌ Myth: Malta eSIMs are expensive compared to local SIM cards.
✅ Fact: eSIM plans are competitively priced and often cheaper than tourist SIM cards from airport kiosks. Plus, you save time and avoid the hassle of finding a store.
