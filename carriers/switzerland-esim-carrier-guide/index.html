⚠️ Common myths and facts about Switzerland eSIM

❌ Myth: Switzerland has the same mobile coverage as the EU.
✅ Fact: False. Switzerland is not part of the EU, and its networks operate independently. Coverage in mountain regions is often patchy, especially for smaller operators like Salt.
❌ Myth: All Swiss operators offer identical speeds.
✅ Fact: Incorrect. Sunrise leads in urban 5G speeds, while Swisscom dominates rural and Alpine areas. Salt is significantly slower outside cities.
