Things you must know before buying a Iraq eSIM

Device compatibility check

Before purchasing an Iraq eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3a and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer models are compatible. Check our eSIM compatibility page for a full list.

Activation & setup

All recommended eSIMs activate via a simple QR code scan. You will receive the QR code by email after purchase. Scan it with your phone’s camera, follow the on-screen instructions, and the eSIM profile will be installed. Upon arrival in Iraq, enable data roaming in your settings – the network will connect automatically. No physical SIM removal or APN configuration is needed.

Coverage & speed expectations

Iraq’s mobile infrastructure is improving, but coverage varies by region. Asiacell offers the best overall 4G/5G coverage, especially in Baghdad, Basra, and the central provinces. Zain Iraq is strong in cities but may be weaker in rural areas. Korek Telecom dominates the Kurdistan Region and remote northern areas. Speeds are generally sufficient for social media, maps, and video calls, but may be slower during peak hours or in very remote locations.

Iraq eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use an eSIM in Iraq with my existing phone number? ▼
    Yes, an eSIM works alongside your physical SIM. You can keep your home number active for calls/SMS while using the Iraq eSIM for data. Just enable data roaming on the eSIM line.
  • Q2: Do I need to register my eSIM with Iraqi authorities? ▼
    No, eSIMs from reputable providers are pre-registered. You do not need to visit any office or provide additional documents.
  • Q3: Will my eSIM work in the Kurdistan Region (Erbil, Sulaymaniyah)? ▼
    Yes, all three operators (Asiacell, Zain Iraq, Korek) cover the Kurdistan Region. Korek Telecom is particularly strong there.
  • Q4: How do I top up my Iraq eSIM if I run out of data? ▼
    Most eSIM providers offer easy online top-ups via their website or app. You can add more data without needing a physical SIM.
  • Q5: Is 5G available in Iraq with eSIMs? ▼
    Yes, Asiacell and Zain Iraq have launched 5G in select areas of Baghdad and other major cities. Your eSIM will automatically connect to 5G when available.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Iraq eSIM

❌ Myth: You need a physical SIM to get online in Iraq – eSIMs are not supported.
✅ Fact: False. All three major Iraqi operators (Asiacell, Zain Iraq, Korek) support eSIM, and many international eSIM providers offer Iraq plans that activate instantly.
❌ Myth: Internet in Iraq is too slow for video calls or streaming.
✅ Fact: Not true in most cities. Asiacell and Zain Iraq offer 4G/5G speeds sufficient for HD video calls and streaming. Rural areas may be slower, but urban connectivity is reliable.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are more expensive than buying a local SIM at the airport.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are often competitively priced and save you time and taxi fare to a store. Many plans are cheaper than tourist SIMs sold at Baghdad International Airport.
❌ Myth: You cannot use an eSIM in the Kurdistan Region due to separate regulations.
✅ Fact: eSIMs from national operators work seamlessly in the Kurdistan Region. Korek Telecom, based in Erbil, actually provides the best coverage there.
❌ Myth: I need to disable my home SIM to use an eSIM in Iraq.
✅ Fact: No, you can keep your home SIM active for calls/texts while using the eSIM for data. Just set the eSIM as your default for mobile data.
