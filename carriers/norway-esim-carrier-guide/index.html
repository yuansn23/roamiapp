Things you must know before buying a Norway eSIM

Device compatibility check

Before purchasing, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most newer models from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. Check our detailed eSIM compatibility list for your specific device model to avoid any surprises.

Activation and setup

All Roami Norway eSIMs feature instant activation with zero wait time. After purchase, you’ll receive a QR code via email. Scan it with your phone’s camera, follow the on-screen instructions, and your eSIM profile will be installed. Once you land in Norway, simply enable the data line and turn on data roaming. Data roaming and APN settings

For both Telenor and Telia eSIMs, data roaming must be enabled on your device. In most cases, the APN is automatically configured. If you encounter connection issues, manually set the APN to “roami” (without quotes). This step is rarely needed but is provided for troubleshooting.

Norway eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use a Norway eSIM if my phone is locked to another carrier? ▼
    Yes, as long as your phone is unlocked. A locked device will only accept SIMs from the original carrier. Check your phone’s settings or contact your carrier to confirm it’s unlocked. For device-specific guidance, refer to our eSIM compatibility page.
  • Q2: Will I get a Norwegian phone number with the eSIM? ▼
    No, Roami Norway eSIMs are data-only. You will not receive a local phone number. For calls and texts, use VoIP apps like WhatsApp, Skype, or FaceTime over the data connection.
  • Q3: How do I avoid roaming fees with a Norway eSIM? ▼
    By using a local Norway eSIM from Telenor or Telia, you connect directly to the local network. This eliminates international roaming charges from your home carrier. Simply activate the eSIM and disable your home SIM’s data roaming to be safe.
  • Q4: Is the Norway eSIM compatible with all phone models? ▼
    Most modern smartphones support eSIM, including iPhone XR and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, and many others. Please verify your device on our eSIM compatibility list before purchasing.
  • Q5: What happens if I run out of data during my trip? ▼
    You can easily top up your eSIM data plan through your Roami account. Top-ups are processed instantly, so you can add more data without any downtime.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Norway eSIM

❌ Myth: Norway has no mobile coverage in the fjords.
✅ Fact: While some deep fjord valleys have limited signal, Telenor’s network covers most populated fjord areas, including popular spots like Geirangerfjord and Nærøyfjord. You’ll have data for navigation and messaging in the vast majority of tourist destinations.
❌ Myth: You need a physical SIM card to get a Norwegian number.
✅ Fact: For data-only needs, an eSIM works perfectly. If you require a local number for calls, some providers offer eSIMs with a number, but for most travelers, data-only eSIMs combined with VoIP apps are sufficient and more cost-effective.
❌ Myth: 5G is available everywhere in Norway.
✅ Fact: 5G is rapidly expanding but is primarily concentrated in major cities like Oslo, Bergen, and Stavanger. In rural and mountainous areas, 4G/LTE remains the dominant and reliable technology. Both Telenor and Telia provide excellent 4G coverage nationwide.
❌ Myth: All eSIMs work instantly without any setup.
✅ Fact: While activation is instant, you must enable data roaming on your device after installing the eSIM profile. Some devices may also require a simple APN configuration. Our setup guide ensures a smooth connection.
❌ Myth: Using an eSIM drains your battery faster than a physical SIM.
✅ Fact: Modern smartphones manage eSIM power consumption efficiently. There is no noticeable difference in battery life between eSIM and physical SIM usage. The network signal strength has a far greater impact on battery drain.
