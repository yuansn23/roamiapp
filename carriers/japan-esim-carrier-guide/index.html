Match Your Itinerary to the Right Network

Japan's diverse terrain demands a tailored network choice. The wrong carrier can leave you without a signal on Mount Fuji or buffer videos in Shibuya Crossing.

🏙️

City Tourism & Digital Nomads

Osaka, Tokyo, Kyoto

Winner: Rakuten Mobile

For uploading photos instantly in Shibuya or taking Zoom calls in an Osaka cafe, Rakuten's Ookla-verified fastest 5G (151.16 Mbps) is unmatched. Osaka is officially the fastest region in Japan for mobile downloads.

While Rakuten is the speed king in urban centers, its coverage can drop in remote mountainous areas, making it best for pure city itineraries.

📱

Entertainment & Social Media Sharing

Shinkansen, Hotels, Cafes

Winner: SoftBank

If your trip involves watching YouTube on the Shinkansen, scrolling TikTok, or playing mobile games at night, SoftBank is your best bet. It officially won Ookla's 'Best Mobile Video Experience' and 'Best Mobile Gaming Experience'.

SoftBank's network is highly optimized for low latency and media parsing, ensuring a buffer-free experience for heavy social media users.

🗻

Rural & Nature Exploration

Japanese Alps, Shirakawa-go, Hokkaido

Winner: NTT DoCoMo

A reliable Japan eSIM is crucial for GPS navigation on remote hiking trails or checking bus schedules in rural villages. NTT DoCoMo provides the highest 5G availability (43%) and the most extensive coverage footprint nationwide.

Using a weaker network in these areas forces your phone to constantly hunt for a signal, leading to rapid battery depletion and potential GPS dropouts.

Japan eSIM FAQs

Get answers to common questions about mobile internet and choosing the right eSIM for your trip to Japan.

Which Japanese mobile carrier has the fastest 5G according to Ookla?
Based on Ookla Speedtest Intelligence data for H2 2024, Rakuten Mobile has the fastest 5G network in Japan, with a median download speed of 151.16 Mbps. Osaka was also recorded as the fastest region overall.

Which network is best for watching videos and playing games in Japan?
SoftBank officially won Ookla’s awards for both ‘Best Mobile Video Experience’ and ‘Best Mobile Gaming Experience’ in H2 2024. If you stream a lot of content while traveling, an eSIM on the SoftBank network is ideal.

Is it better to buy a SIM at Tokyo's Narita Airport or get an eSIM online?
Buying a prepaid Japan eSIM online is far superior. You avoid long lines at airport kiosks, activate instantly upon arrival, and often get access to multiple networks, unlike a single-carrier physical SIM from Narita or Haneda.

Will my Japan travel eSIM automatically switch between Rakuten, DoCoMo, and SoftBank?
Yes, advanced Japan eSIMs from providers like Roami feature intelligent network switching. Your device will automatically connect to the strongest available signal from partnered carriers, giving you Rakuten’s speed in the city and DoCoMo’s coverage in the mountains.
