Things you must know before buying a Japan eSIM

Check your device compatibility

Before purchasing a Japan eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3 and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and later models are compatible. Visit our eSIM compatibility page to verify your device.

Understand data-only vs. voice plans

Most travel eSIMs for Japan are data-only. For voice calls, use apps like WhatsApp, Skype, or LINE. If you need a local number, consider a traditional SIM or a hybrid eSIM that includes voice.

Activation tips

Install your eSIM before departure. Upon arrival in Japan, enable data roaming in your phone settings. The eSIM will automatically connect to the local network. No physical SIM removal is needed.

Japan eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use a Japan eSIM on multiple devices? ▼
    No, an eSIM is tied to a single device. If you need connectivity on a tablet or laptop, consider a separate eSIM or a portable Wi-Fi router.
  • Q2: Will my Japan eSIM work in rural areas like Hokkaido or Okinawa? ▼
    Yes, but coverage varies. NTT DOCOMO offers the best rural coverage. SoftBank is excellent in cities but may be weaker in remote islands. Check coverage maps before choosing.
  • Q3: How do I avoid unexpected roaming charges with a Japan eSIM? ▼
    Remove or disable your home carrier’s physical SIM before departure. Use the eSIM as your primary data line. Ensure data roaming is enabled for the eSIM profile only.
  • Q4: Is 5G available with Japan eSIMs? ▼
    Yes, all three major operators (NTT DOCOMO, SoftBank, KDDI) offer 5G in most cities. Your device must be 5G-capable and support the specific bands used in Japan.
  • Q5: Can I keep my WhatsApp number with a Japan eSIM? ▼
    Yes, WhatsApp works over data. As long as you have an internet connection, your number remains active. No need for a local SIM number.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Japan eSIM

❌ Myth: Japan eSIMs are expensive and only for short trips.
✅ Fact: Many Japan eSIMs are affordable, with plans starting under $10 for a week. They are cost-effective for both short and long stays, especially compared to international roaming.
❌ Myth: All eSIMs in Japan use the same network, so coverage is identical.
✅ Fact: Coverage varies significantly. NTT DOCOMO has the widest rural coverage, while SoftBank excels in cities. KDDI is a budget option but may have gaps in remote areas.
❌ Myth: You need a Japanese phone number to use an eSIM.
✅ Fact: Most travel eSIMs are data-only. You don’t need a local number. Apps like WhatsApp, LINE, and Skype handle calls and texts over data.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are not compatible with older smartphones.
✅ Fact: eSIM technology is available on many older models, including iPhone XR, Google Pixel 3, and Samsung Galaxy S20. Check eSIM compatibility for your device.
❌ Myth: Japan’s internet is slow and unreliable for tourists.
✅ Fact: Japan has one of the fastest and most reliable mobile networks globally. 5G speeds in Tokyo often exceed 100 Mbps. Rural areas still offer solid 4G connectivity.
