Things you must know before buying a Netherlands eSIM

Device compatibility

Before purchasing, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern phones from 2019 onwards are compatible. Check our full list for eSIM compatibility.

Activation process

After buying your eSIM, you’ll receive a QR code via email. Scan it using your phone’s settings (usually under Cellular > Add Cellular Plan). Do this before you travel. Upon arrival in the Netherlands, simply enable data roaming and your eSIM will connect automatically.

Network coverage

The Netherlands has excellent 4G and 5G coverage nationwide. KPN offers the widest rural coverage, while Vodafone provides faster speeds in cities. T-Mobile is a solid budget option. All three operators ensure you stay connected from Amsterdam to the Wadden Islands.

Avoiding roaming fees

Using a local Netherlands eSIM eliminates expensive international roaming charges. You get a Dutch IP address and pay local rates for data, making it ideal for long stays or heavy data users.

Netherlands eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use a Netherlands eSIM if my phone is locked to another carrier? ▼
    Yes, as long as your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM technology. Check our eSIM compatibility page for supported devices.
  • Q2: Will I get a Dutch phone number with the eSIM? ▼
    No, the eSIM is data-only. You will not receive a local phone number. Use apps like WhatsApp, Skype, or FaceTime for calls and messages.
  • Q3: How do I activate the eSIM after purchase? ▼
    You’ll receive a QR code via email. Scan it in your phone’s settings (Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan) before you travel. Once in the Netherlands, enable data roaming and the eSIM will connect automatically.
  • Q4: Is 5G available everywhere in the Netherlands? ▼
    5G is widely available in cities and towns, but rural areas may have 4G fallback. KPN offers the most extensive 5G coverage nationwide, while Vodafone provides faster speeds in urban centers.
  • Q5: Can I keep my home SIM active while using the Netherlands eSIM? ▼
    Yes, you can use dual SIM mode. Keep your home SIM for calls and SMS, and set the Netherlands eSIM as the primary data line to avoid roaming fees.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Netherlands eSIM

❌ Myth: All Dutch networks have identical coverage in rural areas like Giethoorn.
✅ Fact: Coverage varies significantly. KPN has the best rural reach, while Vodafone and T-Mobile may have weaker signals in remote villages.
❌ Myth: You need a physical SIM card to get good data in the Netherlands.
✅ Fact: eSIMs from major operators like KPN and Vodafone provide the same high-speed 5G/4G experience as physical SIMs, with the added convenience of instant activation.
❌ Myth: Free public Wi-Fi in Amsterdam is sufficient for a trip.
✅ Fact: Public Wi-Fi is often slow, insecure, and limited to tourist hubs. A local eSIM ensures fast, secure connectivity everywhere, from museums to countryside bike paths.
❌ Myth: 5G is only available in Amsterdam and Rotterdam.
✅ Fact: 5G coverage extends to many smaller cities like Utrecht, Eindhoven, and even parts of the countryside, especially on the KPN network.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are more expensive than local prepaid SIM cards.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are competitively priced and often cheaper than tourist SIM cards at the airport. They also save time and hassle of finding a store upon arrival.
