Frequently Asked Questions about Global eSIM Carriers & Networks

How do I choose the best eSIM operator for my travel destination?

Choosing the best operator depends on your itinerary. If you are staying in major cities, look for Carriers with the 'Fastest 5G' badge (like T-Mobile in the United States or SoftBank in Japan). If you plan to visit rural areas, national parks, or islands, prioritize Carriers with the 'Widest Coverage' badge (such as Verizon in the US, Docomo in Japan, or Telstra in Australia).

Will my eSIM internet speed be as fast as a local physical SIM card?

Yes! As long as your travel eSIM connects to top-tier local Carriers (such as AIS in Thailand, EE in the United Kingdom, or Orange in Europe), you will enjoy the exact same native 4G LTE or 5G speeds as a local physical SIM card, without paying expensive international roaming fees.

Do these global eSIM Carriers support 5G networks?

Most of our partnered tier-1 Carriers worldwide now support 5G. In countries like South Korea, the United Arab Emirates, Japan, and the United States, 5G is widely available in urban areas. Your device will automatically connect to the 5G network if your phone supports it and you are within the coverage area.

Can my eSIM automatically switch between different Carriers?

It depends on your specific eSIM plan. Many regional and global eSIMs (like our Europe eSIM) are multi-network, meaning they will automatically switch between partnered Carriers (e.g., from Orange to Vodafone) to ensure you always have the strongest signal as you cross borders or move between regions.

Will my mobile connection drop in rural areas, mountains, or islands?

Signal strength in remote areas depends heavily on the local telecom infrastructure. To minimize drops, we highly recommend choosing an eSIM that partners with the dominant local carrier known for rural coverage (e.g., Swisscom in the Swiss Alps, Viettel in rural Vietnam, or Telkomsel for Indonesian islands).
