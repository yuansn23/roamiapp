Things you must know before buying a Albania eSIM

Device compatibility check

Before purchasing an Albania eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3 and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer models are compatible. For a full list, visit our eSIM compatibility page.

How to activate your Albania eSIM

After buying your eSIM, you’ll receive a QR code via email. Go to Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan on iPhone, or Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Add Carrier on Android. Scan the QR code, label the plan (e.g., “Albania Data”), and set it as your data line. Enable Data Roaming for that line. Your home number remains active for calls and texts.

Network coverage in Albania

Albania’s mobile coverage is generally good along the coast and in major cities. Vodafone and Telekom both offer extensive 4G/LTE networks. In remote mountain areas, Telekom has a slight advantage. 5G is not yet widely available in Albania, but 4G speeds are sufficient for streaming, navigation, and social media.

Albania eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I keep my primary number active while using an Albania eSIM? ▼
    Yes. The Albania eSIM is a data-only plan that works alongside your physical SIM or another eSIM. Your primary number remains active for calls and texts on your home network, while the eSIM handles data. This is perfect for avoiding roaming fees and staying reachable on your regular number.
  • Q2: Which Albania eSIM is best for streaming and video calls? ▼
    Vodafone’s eSIM offers the fastest data speeds in urban and coastal areas, making it ideal for streaming Netflix, YouTube, or making WhatsApp video calls. In remote mountain regions, Telekom provides more reliable connectivity, though speeds may be lower.
  • Q3: Is 5G available in Albania? ▼
    5G is not yet widely deployed in Albania. Both Vodafone and Telekom primarily offer 4G/LTE, which is sufficient for most online activities. Speeds are comparable to good 4G in Europe.
  • Q4: How do I activate my Albania eSIM? ▼
    After purchase, you’ll receive a QR code via email. Go to your phone’s settings, add a cellular plan, scan the QR code, and enable data roaming for that line. Activation is automatic upon connecting to the local network. No physical SIM swap is needed.
  • Q5: Can I use the Albania eSIM in other countries? ▼
    This eSIM is designed for use in Albania only. If you’re traveling to neighboring countries like Montenegro, North Macedonia, or Greece, you’ll need a separate eSIM or a regional plan.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Albania eSIM

❌ Myth: Albania has no reliable mobile internet outside of Tirana.
✅ Fact: While coverage is best in cities, both Vodafone and Telekom provide 4G/LTE in most tourist areas, including the Albanian Riviera, Berat, Gjirokastër, and even parts of the Accursed Mountains. Telekom, in particular, reaches many remote villages.
❌ Myth: You need to buy a local SIM card at the airport to get good data.
✅ Fact: An eSIM offers the same network quality as a physical SIM from Vodafone or Telekom, but with the convenience of instant activation before you arrive. You avoid queues and keep your home number active.
❌ Myth: Albanian mobile networks are very slow compared to Western Europe.
✅ Fact: Albania’s 4G/LTE networks are competitive with many European countries. Vodafone’s median download speed in Tirana is comparable to cities like Rome or Lisbon, according to Ookla’s Q3 2024 data.
❌ Myth: eSIMs don’t work in Albania’s mountainous regions.
✅ Fact: eSIMs work exactly like physical SIMs — they connect to the same local towers. Telekom’s eSIM is particularly effective in the Albanian Alps and northern highlands, where its network has been expanded in recent years.
❌ Myth: You must disable your primary SIM to use an Albania eSIM.
✅ Fact: No. You can keep your primary SIM active for calls and texts while using the eSIM for data. Just set the eSIM as your default data line in your phone’s settings. Your home number remains reachable.
