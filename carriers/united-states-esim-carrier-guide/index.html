Things you must know before buying a United States eSIM

Check your device compatibility

Before purchasing, ensure your smartphone is unlocked and supports eSIM. Most recent iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3a and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and later models are compatible. Visit our eSIM compatibility page for a full list.

Understand data roaming settings

After installing your eSIM profile, you must enable Data Roaming in your mobile network settings for the eSIM line. This does not incur extra charges with Roami plans. Disable your primary physical SIM’s data to avoid accidental domestic roaming charges.

Choose the right plan duration

Roami offers 7, 15, and 30-day plans. If you’re visiting multiple cities or national parks, a 30-day plan with hotspot tethering provides the best value. For short business trips, a 7-day plan is sufficient.

United States eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use eSIM in the United States with my current phone? ▼
    Most modern smartphones support eSIM. Check our eSIM compatibility page to confirm your device. Your phone must be unlocked and not carrier-locked to a specific US provider.
  • Q2: Will I be charged roaming fees with a US eSIM? ▼
    No. Roami eSIM plans are prepaid and include data only. There are no surprise roaming fees. You will connect to local networks (T-Mobile, AT&T, or Verizon) without any extra charges.
  • Q3: How do I activate my eSIM after landing in the USA? ▼
    Simply enable the eSIM line in your phone settings and turn on data roaming. Activation is automatic. You do not need to insert a physical SIM. Follow the instructions in your confirmation email.
  • Q4: Which US network offers the best coverage for national parks? ▼
    AT&T generally has the widest rural footprint, making it the best choice for national parks like Yellowstone, Grand Canyon, and the Great Smoky Mountains. T-Mobile and Verizon are also good but may have gaps in very remote areas.
  • Q5: Can I use my US eSIM data for hotspot tethering? ▼
    Yes, all Roami US eSIM plans support hotspot tethering. You can share your data connection with a laptop or tablet. Check your plan details for any fair usage limits.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about United States eSIM

❌ Myth: You need a US phone number to use an eSIM in the United States.
✅ Fact: False. Roami eSIMs are data-only. You do not need a local number. Use WhatsApp, FaceTime, or Skype for calls and texts over data.
❌ Myth: eSIM coverage is the same across all US states.
✅ Fact: Not exactly. Coverage varies by network. T-Mobile excels in cities, AT&T in rural areas, and Verizon in suburban zones. Choose based on your itinerary.
❌ Myth: You must activate your eSIM before leaving for the USA.
✅ Fact: You can install the eSIM profile before departure, but activation only occurs when you connect to a US network. Some travelers prefer to install at the airport upon arrival.
❌ Myth: All US eSIMs are throttled after a few GB.
✅ Fact: Roami plans have generous data caps with clear fair usage policies. You will not be throttled unless you exceed the plan limit. Check your plan details before purchase.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are not secure and can be hacked easily.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are as secure as physical SIMs. They use the same encryption standards and cannot be physically removed or cloned without your device. Always purchase from trusted providers like Roami.
