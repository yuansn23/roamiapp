⚠️ Common myths and facts about Singapore eSIM

❌ Myth: All Singapore mobile networks have the same coverage everywhere.
✅ Fact: While Singapore is small, coverage varies. Singtel leads in urban 5G, but StarHub has better rural reach (e.g., Pulau Ubin). M1 is strong in city centres but may be weaker in remote areas.
❌ Myth: You need to buy a physical SIM at Changi Airport to get cheap data.
✅ Fact: eSIMs are often cheaper and more convenient. You can purchase and install one before your flight, avoiding airport queues and premium prices.
❌ Myth: Singapore’s 5G is only available in the city centre.
✅ Fact: All three major operators have rolled out 5G across most of the island, including residential areas and tourist spots like Sentosa and Gardens by the Bay. Rural areas may still rely on 4G.
❌ Myth: eSIMs drain your battery faster than physical SIMs.
✅ Fact: Modern smartphones manage eSIM power efficiently. Battery drain is negligible compared to the convenience of having instant local connectivity without swapping SIMs.
❌ Myth: You need to register your eSIM with Singapore authorities before use.
✅ Fact: No registration is required for tourists using eSIMs. The operator handles all compliance. Just install and activate – you’re good to go.
