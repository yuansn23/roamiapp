Claro Argentina eSIM Review: Best 5G Speed for Urban Travelers

  • Best for: Travelers who prioritize fast, reliable data in cities and want the best 5G experience for streaming, video calls, and navigation without buffering.
  • Core data: Claro is Argentina’s largest mobile operator by subscribers and consistently ranks #1 in Ookla’s Speedtest for 5G availability and median download speeds in Buenos Aires, Córdoba, and Rosario.
  • Licensed by ENACOM (Ente Nacional de Comunicaciones). Operates on 700 MHz (4G), 1900 MHz, and 3500 MHz (5G) bands.
eSIM connection note: eSIM activates automatically upon arrival. Ensure data roaming is enabled in your device settings. No APN configuration required for most devices.

Movistar Argentina eSIM Review: Best Coverage for Remote Adventures

  • Best for: Adventure travelers, hikers, and road-trippers who need reliable connectivity in Patagonia, the Andes, and other remote areas where other networks struggle.
  • Core data: Movistar (Telefónica) has the widest 4G/LTE coverage footprint in rural and mountainous regions of Argentina, including national parks like Los Glaciares and Nahuel Huapi.
  • Licensed by ENACOM. Operates on 850 MHz, 1900 MHz, and 700 MHz bands, with 5G rolling out in major cities.
eSIM connection note: eSIM activates upon landing. Turn on data roaming. For best performance, manually select Movistar network in remote areas to avoid roaming onto weaker partner networks.

✨ Real‑world performance


  • Hiking in Patagonia: Check trail conditions and share your location with family while trekking in Torres del Paine (Chile side) or El Chaltén – Movistar’s signal reaches deeper into the wilderness.
    - Road trip along Ruta 40: Stream music and use offline maps for the long stretches between Bariloche and El Calafate without losing connection.
    - Cruise to Ushuaia: Stay in touch with friends while exploring the End of the World – Movistar provides the most consistent signal in Tierra del Fuego.


Personal Argentina eSIM Review: Budget-Friendly Data for Short Stays

  • Core data: Personal (Telecom Argentina) offers competitive prepaid eSIM plans with good 4G+ speeds in urban centers. It is a reliable choice for cost-conscious travelers who don’t require the fastest 5G.
  • Licensed by ENACOM. Operates on 1900 MHz, 700 MHz, and AWS bands. 5G is available in select Buenos Aires neighborhoods.
