Understanding Nicaragua’s mobile landscape

Nicaragua has two main mobile operators: Claro and Tigo. Both offer prepaid eSIM options through Roami. Claro has the widest rural coverage, while Tigo excels in urban areas. 3G is still present in remote zones, but 4G/LTE is the standard in cities and tourist spots. 5G is not yet widely deployed.

Nicaragua eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use an eSIM in Nicaragua if my phone is locked to a US carrier? ▼
    Most US carrier-locked phones (e.g., T-Mobile, AT&T, Verizon) do not allow adding a foreign eSIM. You need an unlocked device. Check our eSIM compatibility page for details.
  • Q2: Will I get 5G speeds in Nicaragua? ▼
    5G is not yet commercially available in Nicaragua. You will connect to 4G/LTE networks, which are sufficient for streaming, video calls, and social media.
  • Q3: How do I top up my Nicaragua eSIM? ▼
    Roami eSIMs are data-only and prepaid. You can purchase additional data packages through your Roami account before or during your trip.
  • Q4: Can I keep my WhatsApp number with a Nicaragua eSIM? ▼
    Yes. As long as your primary SIM is active for SMS verification (or you verify via email), WhatsApp will continue to work with your usual number.
  • Q5: Is there a risk of throttling after using a lot of data? ▼
    Roami eSIMs offer fixed data allowances without throttling. Once you exhaust your plan, you can buy a new package to continue at full speed.
