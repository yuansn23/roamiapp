⚠️ Common myths and facts about Greece eSIM

❌ Myth: All Greek islands have the same mobile coverage.
✅ Fact: Coverage varies significantly. Cosmote has the widest island network, while other operators may have weaker signals on smaller or less touristy islands.
❌ Myth: You need a physical SIM card to get good data in Greece.
✅ Fact: eSIMs work just as well as physical SIMs and often provide faster activation. Many travelers now prefer eSIMs for convenience.
❌ Myth: 5G is available everywhere in Greece.
