Things you must know before buying a Ireland eSIM

Check Your Device Compatibility

Before purchasing an Ireland eSIM, verify that your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most recent models from Apple (iPhone XR and newer), Google Pixel (3a and newer), and Samsung Galaxy (S20 and newer) are compatible. Visit our eSIM compatibility page to confirm your device.

How to Activate Your Ireland eSIM


  1. Purchase and receive your eSIM QR code via email.
    2. Go to Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan on your iPhone, or Settings > Connections > SIM card manager > Add eSIM on Android.
    3. Scan the QR code and label the plan as ‘Ireland Data’.
    4. Upon landing in Ireland, enable Data Roaming for this line. The eSIM will automatically connect to the local network.
    5. No physical SIM removal is required – keep your home SIM active for calls and texts if needed.

⚠️ Common myths and facts about Ireland eSIM

❌ Myth: All eSIMs in Ireland use the same network, so coverage is identical.
✅ Fact: False. Vodafone and Three operate separate networks. Vodafone has wider rural coverage (e.g., Connemara, Ring of Kerry), while Three offers faster speeds in cities. Choosing the right carrier matters for your itinerary.
❌ Myth: You need a physical Irish SIM to get a local number for calls.
✅ Fact: Not true. Roami’s eSIM provides data-only connectivity, which is perfect for WhatsApp, Skype, and FaceTime Audio. For calls to local numbers, you can use VoIP apps – no physical SIM required.
❌ Myth: Ireland’s mobile coverage is poor outside of Dublin.
✅ Fact: While coverage is densest in cities, Vodafone’s network reaches 99% of the population, including most tourist spots like the Cliffs of Moher and Killarney National Park. Remote hiking trails may have gaps, but main roads and towns are well covered.
❌ Myth: eSIMs are more expensive than buying a local SIM at the airport.
✅ Fact: Roami’s eSIM plans are competitively priced and often cheaper than airport SIM kiosks. Plus, you avoid the hassle of finding a store, paying with cash, or dealing with activation in a foreign language.
❌ Myth: You need to remove your home SIM to use an eSIM in Ireland.
✅ Fact: No. eSIM works alongside your physical SIM. You can keep your home SIM active for calls/texts and use the eSIM solely for data. This is a key advantage of eSIM technology for travelers.
