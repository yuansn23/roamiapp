⚠️ Common myths and facts about Lithuania eSIM

❌ Myth: Lithuania’s mobile networks are slow and unreliable outside Vilnius.
✅ Fact: Telia and Bitė provide excellent 4G and 5G coverage even in rural areas like the Curonian Spit and Aukštaitija National Park. Speeds are comparable to Western European standards.
❌ Myth: You need a Lithuanian phone number to use mobile data.
✅ Fact: No, eSIM data-only plans work perfectly without a local number. You can use WhatsApp, Skype, or other VoIP apps for calls.
❌ Myth: Free public WiFi in Lithuania is safe for business.
✅ Fact: Public WiFi in cafes and airports can be insecure. A personal eSIM with Telia or Bitė provides a private, encrypted connection, protecting sensitive business data.
❌ Myth: All eSIMs in Lithuania offer the same speed and coverage.
✅ Fact: Coverage and speed vary significantly. Telia leads in rural coverage, Bitė in urban speed, and Tele2 offers budget options. Choose based on your itinerary.
