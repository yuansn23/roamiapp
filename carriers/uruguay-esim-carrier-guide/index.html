Before purchasing an eSIM for Uruguay, confirm that your smartphone is unlocked and supports eSIM compatibility. Most recent iPhone models (XR and newer), Google Pixel (3a and newer), and Samsung Galaxy (S20 and newer) are compatible. Older devices may require a physical SIM.

Uruguay eSIMs from Roami are pre-activated – simply install the eSIM profile before departure. Upon arrival, enable data roaming in your phone’s settings. No physical SIM removal is needed. If you encounter issues, restart your device or toggle airplane mode.

Network coverage considerations

Antel offers the widest coverage, especially in coastal areas and along highways. Claro is excellent in cities but may be weaker in remote rural regions. For trips that include both urban and countryside destinations, Antel is the safer choice.

Uruguay eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use an eSIM in Uruguay if my phone is locked to a carrier? ▼
    No, your phone must be unlocked to use a local eSIM. Check with your current carrier before traveling. For a list of compatible devices, see our eSIM compatibility page.
  • Q2: Will I lose my home number if I switch to a Uruguay eSIM? ▼
    No. You can keep your physical SIM active for calls/texts while using the eSIM for data. Just enable ‘Cellular Data’ on the eSIM line in your settings.
  • Q3: How do I top up my Uruguay eSIM data plan? ▼
    Roami eSIMs come with a fixed data allowance. If you need more, you can purchase a new eSIM plan online and install it as a second profile. No physical top-up needed.
  • Q4: Is 5G available everywhere in Uruguay? ▼
    5G is primarily available in Montevideo, Punta del Este, and other major urban centers. In rural areas, you’ll typically get 4G LTE, which is still fast for most tasks.
  • Q5: Can I use a Uruguay eSIM for tethering or hotspot? ▼
    Yes, most Roami eSIM plans allow hotspot usage. However, check the specific plan’s fair use policy to avoid throttling after heavy usage.

❌ Myth: You need a local ID to buy a prepaid eSIM in Uruguay.
✅ Fact: Not true for Roami eSIMs. They are available to international travelers without registration. Just purchase online and install the profile before your trip.

✅ Fact: Roami eSIMs have a flexible activation window. You can install the profile before departure, and it will activate automatically when you connect to a supported network in Uruguay. No rush.
