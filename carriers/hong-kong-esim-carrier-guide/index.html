Which mobile network is the fastest in Hong Kong according to Ookla?
Based on Ookla Speedtest Intelligence data for H1 2025, China Mobile Hong Kong is the fastest mobile provider, recording a median overall download speed of 119.24 Mbps and a median 5G download speed of 185.43 Mbps.

Which network is the most reliable for traveling around Hong Kong?
csl recorded the best mobile network Consistency in Hong Kong. 92.5% of its overall samples and 85.5% of its 5G samples met or exceeded high-performance thresholds, making it incredibly reliable on the go.

What is the best fixed broadband provider in Hong Kong?
Netvigator Home Broadband is the top-performing fixed ISP, winning awards for Fastest Fixed ISP (438.06 Mbps median download), Best Fixed Network, and Best ISP Gaming Experience.

Do I need an eSIM if Hong Kong has widespread public Wi-Fi?
Yes! While Hong Kong has excellent fixed Wi-Fi (often powered by Netvigator), you will need an eSIM for mobile data to use digital payment apps, navigate the MTR, and access real-time translation tools while exploring the city.
