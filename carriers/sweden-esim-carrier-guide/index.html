Things you must know before buying a Sweden eSIM

Check Your Device Compatibility

Before purchasing a Sweden eSIM, ensure your smartphone is unlocked and supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels, and Samsung Galaxy S20+ models work. Visit our eSIM compatibility page for a full list.

Understand Local Network Bands

Sweden uses 4G bands 1, 3, 7, 8, 20, and 5G bands n78, n28. All major eSIM providers support these, but older Chinese or US-only phones may lack band 20 (important for rural coverage). Check your device specs to avoid connectivity gaps.

Activation and Setup Tips

Most Sweden eSIMs activate automatically upon landing. To avoid issues: install the eSIM profile before your trip, enable data roaming in settings, and ensure your primary SIM is turned off or set to data roaming off. No physical SIM swap needed.

Sweden eSIM frequently asked questions

  • Q1: Can I use a Sweden eSIM immediately after landing? ▼
    Yes, most Sweden eSIMs activate automatically upon arrival. Ensure you install the eSIM profile before your trip and enable data roaming in your device settings.
  • Q2: Will a Sweden eSIM work in the Stockholm archipelago? ▼
    Telia eSIM offers the best coverage in the archipelago. Telenor and Tre have limited coverage on smaller islands, so choose Telia if you plan to explore beyond the city.
  • Q3: Which Sweden eSIM is best for video calls? ▼
    Telenor eSIM provides the highest 5G speeds in urban areas, making it ideal for smooth Zoom or FaceTime calls in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö.
  • Q4: Do I need to set up an APN for Sweden eSIM? ▼
    For Telia and Telenor eSIMs, no APN configuration is needed. For Tre eSIM, some devices may require setting the APN to ‘3.se’. Check our eSIM compatibility guide for details.
  • Q5: Can I keep my home SIM active while using Sweden eSIM? ▼
    Yes, you can use dual SIM mode. Just set the Sweden eSIM as the primary data line and turn off data roaming on your home SIM to avoid charges.
  • Q6: Is there a Sweden eSIM with unlimited data? ▼
    Some providers offer high-data caps (e.g., 50GB), but truly unlimited plans are rare. Check the plan details before purchasing to ensure it meets your needs.
