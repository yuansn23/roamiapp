1. Purchase & Delivery

What is a travel eSIM and how does it work?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built directly into your smartphone. Unlike physical SIM cards, you don't need to insert or swap anything. You simply scan a QR code to download a data plan, allowing you to connect to local mobile networks instantly when traveling abroad.

How exactly is the eSIM delivered after I pay?

Delivery is 100% digital and instant. The moment your payment is successfully processed, we automatically send an email containing your eSIM QR code, manual activation codes, and a step-by-step installation guide. You can also access your eSIM directly from your Roami account dashboard.

Do I need to provide my Passport or ID (KYC)?

No, absolutely not. We value your privacy. Roami travel eSIMs are completely No-KYC (Know Your Customer). You do not need to upload your passport, ID card, or complete any real-name registration. You can purchase and use our data plans anonymously.

What payment methods do you accept?

We offer a highly secure checkout process supporting multiple global payment methods. You can pay using major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as PayPal.

When is the best time to buy my eSIM?

We recommend purchasing and installing your eSIM 1 to 3 days before your departure. You need a stable Wi-Fi connection to install the eSIM. Since the plan only activates when you arrive at your destination, buying it early ensures you have internet access the moment your plane lands.

Do I get a local phone number with my eSIM?

Most of our travel eSIMs are Data-Only plans, meaning they do not come with a local phone number for traditional voice calls or SMS. However, you can easily use VoIP apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime, Skype, Telegram, or Messenger to make voice and video calls using your eSIM data.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard Stripe and PayPal payment gateways. Your credit card details are fully encrypted and never stored on our servers. Our website is secured with SSL encryption to ensure your data is perfectly safe.

2. Compatibility & Devices

What are the basic requirements for my phone to use an eSIM?

Your device must meet two absolute conditions:
1. It must be carrier-unlocked (not tied to a specific network provider contract).
2. It must have built-in eSIM hardware.
You can usually check this by going to your phone's settings under 'Cellular' or 'Network' to see if there is an 'Add eSIM' or 'Add Data Plan' option.

Can I use an eSIM if my phone is locked to a carrier?

No. If your phone is locked to a specific carrier (common with contract phones in the US, UK, or Japan), you will not be able to install a third-party travel eSIM. Please contact your home carrier to request an unlock before purchasing.

Can I use my physical SIM and eSIM at the same time?

Yes! Most modern smartphones support Dual SIM functionality (Physical SIM + eSIM). You can keep your home physical SIM active to receive important SMS (like bank OTPs) while using the Roami eSIM exclusively for mobile data. Just make sure to turn off Data Roaming on your home SIM to avoid expensive charges.

Can I transfer my eSIM to a new phone?

No. For security reasons, an eSIM QR code can only be scanned and installed once. It binds to the device it is first installed on. You cannot transfer the eSIM to another device, so please ensure you install it on the phone you intend to travel with.

3. Installation & Setup

How do I install the eSIM on my iPhone (iOS)?

1. Connect to Wi-Fi.
2. Go to Settings > Cellular (or Mobile Data).
3. Tap Add eSIM or Add Data Plan.
4. Select 'Use QR Code' and scan the code we emailed you.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to label the plan (e.g., 'Travel').

How do I install the eSIM on my Android device?

1. Connect to Wi-Fi.
2. Go to Settings > Connections > SIM Manager.
3. Tap Add Mobile Plan or Add eSIM.
4. Select 'Scan Carrier QR Code' and scan the code.
5. Confirm to add the plan to your device.

I can't scan the QR code because it's on my phone. What do I do?

If you receive the QR code on the phone you want to install it on, you have two options:
1. Send the QR code to another device (like a laptop, iPad, or a friend's phone) and scan it from that screen.
2. Use the Manual Installation method. Copy the SM-DP+ Address and Activation Code provided in your email and paste them into the manual entry fields in your phone's eSIM settings.

Do I need a Wi-Fi connection to install the eSIM?

Yes. Your phone needs an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or your home cellular data) to download the eSIM profile from the carrier's server. We highly recommend installing it at home or at the airport before you fly.

I accidentally deleted my eSIM. Can I reinstall it?

No. Once an eSIM is deleted from your device, it is permanently removed and the QR code becomes invalid. Never delete your eSIM if you are experiencing connection issues; deleting it will not fix the issue. If you accidentally deleted it, please contact support immediately.

Should I turn on the eSIM immediately after installation?

You can leave the eSIM turned off after installation. Wait until you arrive at your destination country to toggle the line 'ON' and enable Data Roaming. This prevents any accidental usage or activation before your trip begins.

4. Activation & Usage

When does my eSIM data plan actually start activating?

The validity period of your eSIM (e.g., 7 days, 15 days) only begins when the eSIM connects to the supported local network at your destination. Installing the eSIM at home does NOT start the timer. You have full control over when the plan activates.

Can I share my data or use a mobile hotspot?

Yes, absolutely! All Roami eSIMs fully support mobile hotspot and tethering. You can easily share your internet connection with your laptop, tablet, or travel companions. Just turn on the 'Personal Hotspot' feature in your phone's settings.

How do I turn on the eSIM when I arrive?

When you land, go to Settings > Cellular.
1. Turn ON the Roami eSIM line.
2. Set the Roami eSIM as the primary line for Cellular Data.
3. Ensure Data Roaming is toggled ON specifically for the Roami eSIM.

Why is my eSIM not connecting to the internet?

If you have no internet, please check the following:
- Is the eSIM turned ON?
- Is it selected for Cellular Data?
- Is Data Roaming turned ON?
- Try turning Airplane Mode on and off, or restart your phone.
If it still doesn't work, you may need to manually select a network or configure the APN (details provided in your setup email).

Do I need to turn on Data Roaming?

Yes. Travel eSIMs operate on roaming networks. You must turn on 'Data Roaming' for the eSIM line in your settings for it to work. Don't worry, as long as you are using the prepaid Roami eSIM for data, you will not incur any roaming charges from your home carrier.
