How to check if your phone is eSIM compatible?

Before purchasing a travel data plan, it's crucial to verify that your device is unlocked and supports eSIM technology. The quickest way to check is by looking for an EID number.

  • Open your phone's dialer app.
  • Dial *#06# and press call.
  • If your device is compatible, an EID number (a 32-digit code) will appear on your screen.
